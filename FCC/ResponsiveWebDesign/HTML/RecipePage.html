Traditional Slovak Bryndzové Halušky

Discover the taste of Slovakia with this authentic recipe for Bryndzové Halušky. These potato dumplings mixed with creamy sheep cheese (bryndza) and topped with crispy bacon represent the ultimate comfort food that has been cherished for generations.

Ingredients

  • 800g Starchy potatoes
  • 300g All-purpose flour
  • 250g Bryndza (sheep milk cheese)
  • 150g Smoked bacon (slab bacon)
  • A pinch of salt

Instructions

  1. Peel and finely grate the raw potatoes into a large bowl.
  2. Add flour and a pinch of salt to the grated potatoes, mixing until you form a thick, sticky batter.
  3. Drop small pieces of the batter into a pot of boiling salted water using a halušky strainer or a wooden board and knife.
  4. Once the dumplings float to the surface, cook for another 2 minutes, then drain and mix immediately with the bryndza cheese.
  5. Fry the diced bacon until crispy and pour the hot bacon fat and bits over the served halušky.
